Plan Questions for a Career Experience

Build a short list of questions that helps you learn what you actually want to know — whether you are having an informational interview, shadowing someone, talking with a professional, or preparing questions to ask an employer during an interview.

You do not need to ask every question. A good conversation usually starts with a few priorities and leaves room to follow what you learn.
